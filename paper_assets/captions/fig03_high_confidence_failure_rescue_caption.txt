Fig. 3. High-confidence behavior of unseen attack families and EcoCon-NOS rescue. (a) Percentage of outer test-unknown samples assigned at least 0.95 maximum predicted-class probability by the frozen XGBoost classifier despite the family being absent from training. (b) Percentage of those high-confidence unknown samples rejected by EcoCon-NOS. Severe overconfidence is observed for DDoS, DoS, and Mirai; EcoCon-NOS rejects substantial fractions of the high-confidence DDoS and Mirai samples, whereas DoS remains difficult.
